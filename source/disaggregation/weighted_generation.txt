WEIGHTED GENERATION: THE NUMBERS BEHIND THE MARKETING TABLES

SUMMARY

The panel already says which channel and CAC each customer has. 
This disaggregateion works backwards: it splits each channel into
platforms, and invents click and impression counts that fit the CAC. Every
split below is a weighted random draw. Seed is 42, so reruns match.

HOW CUSTOMERS ARE ROUTED

  Channel in panel     Platform table                  Rule
  Ads                  google_ads / facebook_ads       60% Google, 40% Facebook
  Organic              hubspot_organic_downloads       all
  Referral             rewardful_referrals             all
  anything else        dropped, with a warning

THE WEIGHTS

Google Ads
  Ad group             85% brand search, 15% competitor search
  Click-through rate   brand 15.0%, competitor 1.8%
  Noise                each rate x a random 0.90 to 1.10

Facebook Ads
  Ad set               70% lookalike_1pct, 30% broad_b2b_tech
  Placement            65% instagram_feed, 35% facebook_stories
  Click-through rate   3.5% base; x1.4 for lookalike, x0.8 for broad

Both ad platforms
  Clicks               random draw (Poisson) around 50, which is
                       1 / 2% conversion rate. One conversion per customer.
  Impressions          clicks / click-through rate
  Spend                the customer's CAC, rounded to cents

HubSpot organic: first touch by plan tier
  Tier         Viewed Pricing   Downloaded SaaS Model   Blog Subscription
  Enterprise   80%              15%                     5%
  Pro          65%              25%                     10%
  Basic        55%              30%                     15%
  Idea: bigger buyers arrive with buying intent; small ones arrive via content.

Rewardful referrals
  Affiliate id         random 100 to 999. Commission = the customer's CAC.

Fallback: if the panel has no plan_tier column, tiers are drawn at
60% Basic, 30% Pro, 10% Enterprise.

Dates: each conversion lands on a random day and second inside the
customer's cohort month.

WHAT THE WEIGHTS DO TO THE RESULT

- Google's blended click-through rate comes out near 12.9%
  (0.85 x 15.0% + 0.15 x 1.8%).
- Every ad customer costs exactly their CAC, so platform spend adds up to
  the panel's paid CAC. Only clicks and impressions are invented.

LIMITATIONS

- All of these numbers are simply assumptions.  Do not treat as industry benchmarks.
- B2B paid spend breakdown is roughly **46% Google, 41% LinkedIn, 8% Meta** [(**Swydo**)](https://www.swydo.com/blog/google-ads-vs-linkedin-ads/). Google and Meta (no LinkedIn) 60/40 split over-weights Meta. This is a deliberate choice to simplify.
- The 15% brand-search rate is high next to typical paid search averages.
- Other partners are excluded for simplicity, ex. LinkedIn, Bing, partner networks.
